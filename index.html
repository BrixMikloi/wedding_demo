Our Story

Some paths are meant to cross at the perfect time.

John and Arlyn’s story began with a simple conversation that unexpectedly turned into something special.

What started as casual chats slowly became daily conversations, shared dreams, and endless laughter. Through every season of life, they became each other’s comfort, best friend, and safe place.

As time passed, love naturally grew between them — steady, genuine, and full of understanding. Now, their story continues hand in hand, building a future filled with love, memories, and forever.

The Journey

From simple moments to a lifetime together.

During a quiet getaway together, John and Arlyn found themselves surrounded by a breathtaking view and the kind of peace that makes time slow down.

As they watched the scenery and shared stories about their journey together, John held Arlyn’s hand and asked the question he had been carrying in his heart for so long, to spend forever with him.

With happy tears and a heartfelt yes, that beautiful moment became the beginning of their forever.
